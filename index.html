Manual
Savior 505 Loop Station
Tracks
Tap a padCycles rec → play → overdub. The first track you record sets the loop length — every other track auto-syncs to it.
MUTE buttonSilences or resumes that track without losing what's recorded.
Hold a padClears that track for a fresh recording.
Track FX
REVReverses the track's audio, swapped in cleanly at the next loop boundary.
CRUSHSends the track through a lo-fi filter + bitcrush effect.
Knobs
MonitorHow much of your live mic you hear back. Off by default — turn up carefully, and use headphones to avoid feedback.
MasterOverall output volume.
SpaceReverb amount on the master mix.
Small knob per trackThat track's individual level.
Rhythm & Tempo
RHYTHMToggles a metronome click guide, independent of your loops.
TAPTap a few times in tempo to set the BPM.
Memory
Save Mem / Load MemStores or recalls all 5 tracks and their settings in one of 4 slots. Use the ◀ ▶ arrows to pick a slot.
Session onlyMemory slots reset when the page reloads — they don't save to disk.
Transport
Play AllPauses or resumes every track together, in sync.
Hold: New LoopClears everything and starts a fresh loop from scratch.
Tips
New tracks sound blended with old ones?That's the mic picking up earlier tracks playing out of your speaker while you record — not a bug. Wear headphones and it clears right up, since the mic then only hears you.
